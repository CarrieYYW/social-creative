# Hooks that fit any video

Use when writing the opening of a reel, short or script. Production and evidence live with video-editor when available. Virality cannot be promised; a strong opening raises the odds.

## What every hook does

No single line works everywhere. The structure does. In about the first second or two:

1. **Make the subject clear.** The viewer knows what this is and that it is for them.
2. **Open a real question.** A contradiction, a result without its cause, a feeling recognised, something unfinished. The gap should be neither obvious nor impossible to guess.
3. **Say it through every channel.** First frame, on-screen words and first spoken line carry one idea.

Then pay it off. A fake gap costs trust.

Write it as: **show [who or what this is for] in [a state of tension] so the viewer needs [the payoff].** Start with the subject or situation. Skip empty channel introductions, greetings or logos that delay it. An in-scene greeting can stay when the first frame already establishes the situation; judge it in the complete opening.

## Seven types

| Type | Mechanism | Loud | Quiet |
|---|---|---|---|
| `result-first` | Show the end, withhold how | "We sold out in nine minutes." | The finished room, then how it began |
| `contradiction` | Break a held belief | "You don't need more discipline." | "Rest isn't the opposite of work." |
| `recognition` | Name an unsaid feeling | "If you reread texts before sending them…" | One exact, private-feeling line |
| `question` | Ask what they already wonder | "Why do Sundays feel like this?" | A question left open on screen |
| `stakes` | Show what is at risk | "Forty tickets left." | "The last summer in this building." |
| `mid-action` | Drop in as it happens | Mid-sentence | A hand already reaching for the door |
| `image` | A picture needing explanation | An odd frame | One still, strange image |

A quiet brand can still hook hard: a hook needs tension, not volume.

## Write the rest

- Link beats with "but" or "therefore", not "and then".
- Give each beat one job. Cut beats that change nothing.
- Name the payoff before writing. If you cannot, the idea is not ready.
- Name the share reason: what is someone saying when they send this?
- End when the payoff lands.

For a test, keep the body fixed and change only the opening. See [creative testing](creative-testing.md).

Do not use unverified retention percentages, fixed "3-second" cutoffs, or manufactured outrage. Claims in a hook must be true and supportable.
