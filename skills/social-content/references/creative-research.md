## Cross-source synthesis

Our synthesis is a working method: **inspect evidence → extract a pattern → adapt it to the brand → produce and inspect → test a clear hypothesis when useful → retain the outcome**. This combines complementary ideas from the sources; it does not establish a universally successful formula.

| Synthesized lesson | Source basis | What it changes in our work | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learn from existing work before starting over | Buzzfy's reuse loop; Ootto's reference and analytics templates | Inspect relevant assets and comparable account results; preserve the original before revising | A past winner may not fit a new goal, audience, or context |
| Separate the message from its presentation | One Peak's format library; Ootto's format-specific templates | Store reusable format cards with purpose, progression, resolution, treatment, and production needs | Transfer the structure with brand-specific adaptation; do not copy an identity |
| Coordinate words, visuals, and timing | One Peak's opening emphasis; Ootto's beat and shot planning | Plan the opening and body as a coherent communication experience, then inspect actual output | Our adaptation; fast cuts, constant motion, and sensational language are not requirements |
| Make variations answer a question | Buzzfy's revisions; Ootto's hook testing | State a hypothesis and changed/fixed elements; identify when a whole package is being tested | Non-random organic comparisons are directional; changing several elements cannot isolate their effects |
| Define success before interpreting numbers | Runneth's explicit spend definition; Ootto's goal-led analysis | Name the metric, denominator, baseline, population, and observation window | Attention, spend, profitability, comprehension, and craft are different outcomes |
| Retain context with the result | Buzzfy's review loop; Ootto's outcome tracking; our project-memory approach | Save artifacts, feedback, actual results, limits, and the next decision with the active project | A preference is not measured performance; one brand's result does not prove a general rule |

The transferable method belongs in the existing skills and their working references. Product descriptions, benchmark definitions, rejected tactics, unreviewed links, and speculative examples stay in this notebook. The skill instructions now implement the method; audience performance remains to be evaluated through actual use.

## Reference batch: 2026-11-02

### Buzzfy — iteration on existing content

Sources: [product page](https://buzzfy.co/) and [public workflows](https://buzzfy.co/workflows).

**Observed:** Buzzfy describes selecting a creator's successful existing reels, changing openings, subtitles, backgrounds, or reply stickers, then reviewing results. Its baseline uses the creator's own typical performance. Its workflows connect account analysis with ideas and opening variants.

**Useful adaptation:** keep an original/control, identify why a revision is worth trying, and compare results with a relevant account baseline. Revisit good existing material before commissioning entirely new production.

**Limits / not adopted:** the vendor's reported outcomes are self-reported. Its daily posting volume is not a generic recommendation. Comparisons that change multiple elements cannot isolate the effect of captions. Do not present an invented comment as real audience feedback, even when using a reply-card visual treatment.

**Best home:** experiment planning in social-content; controlled revisions in video-editor. Keep product-specific tactics and claims in this notebook.

### One Peak Creative — formats as reusable structures

Sources: [One Peak Creative](https://www.onepeakcreative.com/), [Format Finder](https://formatfinder.onepeakcreative.com/), [TikTok and Reels course page](https://findyourpeak.onepeakcreative.com/tiktok-and-reels-course), and [Art of Hooks course page](https://findyourpeak.onepeakcreative.com/art-of-hooks-web).

**Observed:** the public offering organizes ideas around repeatable formats matched to a niche, connects hooks and scripts with filming guidance, and emphasizes openings. Examples include personal stories, educational explanations, and comparison challenges.

**Useful adaptation:** distinguish the topic from its presentation format. A format card can preserve the opening promise, progression, payoff, visual treatment, production needs, and situations where the format does not fit.

**Limits / not adopted:** the public sales pages do not reveal all paid lessons or prove guaranteed outcomes. Estimated retention in a demo is not measured audience behavior. Do not impose a sensational opening or withhold useful information merely to imitate a format.

**Best home:** project format libraries supported by the existing creative roles. Our format-card guidance is an adaptation, not a verified transcription of One Peak's private method.

### Runneth — understand the benchmark before applying it

Sources: [Creative Benchmarks 2026](https://runneth.com/thumbstop-pulse/creative-benchmarks-2026) and [methodology and definitions](https://runneth.com/thumbstop-pulse/cb2026-methodology-and-definitions).

**Observed:** the report analyzes paid Meta creatives launched September 1, 2025–January 1, 2026, including the holiday advertising period. Its primary outcome is realized spend. A winner must receive at least 10 times the account's median spend and at least $500. The methodology explicitly distinguishes this measure from ROAS or revenue.

**Useful adaptation:** document the objective, paid/organic context, population, time window, metric definition, and limitations before borrowing a benchmark. Compare relevant cohorts within an account. A portfolio of experiments can help learning, but the report does not establish a universal production quota.

**Limits / not adopted:** spending allocation does not establish profitability or creative quality. Paid-ad format rankings are not evidence that organic quote posts or carousels are unsuitable. Do not infer a causal benefit from a correlation between volume and winner frequency.

**Best home:** benchmarking context in this notebook; evidence-aware performance analysis in social-content.

## Retained ideas that are not separate skills

Candidate format structures to explore with real source material:

- **Quote → interpretation → application:** keep exact quotation and attribution separate from commentary. A standalone post, carousel, or animated passage may fit, depending on the content.
- **Question → mechanism → example → takeaway:** a possible structure for an explanatory carousel or reel.
- **Talking head → demonstration → resolution:** keep the speaker visible where expression matters; supporting footage or animation can explain, establish context, or show evidence.

These are untested starting ideas, not fixed recipes. Length, slide count, pace, motion, and any CTA follow the active message and brand. Keep actual format cards and experiments with the project.
