# Social Marketing Learnings

Last updated: 2026-11-02

This document retains useful marketing lessons separately from design and editing craft. The project-research proposal is implemented as social-marketer in version 1.3.0; working instructions live with that skill. Source inspection coverage and the larger synthesis live in [Creative research and learnings](creative-research.md).

## Evaluate marketing lessons as hypotheses

For each useful tactic, retain its source and date, what was actually observed, audience/goal, why it might help, applicability limits, and a way to evaluate it. Distinguish a visible marketing mechanism from verified business performance. A polished demo, vendor testimonial, engagement count, or predicted score does not establish profitable acquisition or an audience benefit.

Keep broadly transferable lessons here. Actual brand strategy, customer language, source assets, results, and decisions belong with that project. Apply [Creative Testing and Learning](creative-testing.md) for experiment design rather than duplicating its method here.

## Marketing lessons from the reference set

| Lesson to evaluate | Source and observation | Application and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Demonstrate the value concretely | [Joseph's earlier tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5UnK3c-nlIs) introduces a raw-to-edited result and explains the workflow | A before/after or worked example can make an offer understandable. Use genuine assets and outcomes; clarity is not proof of conversion. |
| Connect a useful next step to the content | [Ootto](https://ootto.ai/) presents keyword-triggered resource delivery alongside content production | Offer a relevant resource or next action when it serves the goal. A contemplative quote may need no CTA. Resource fulfillment and account actions require explicit execution instructions. |
| Build on relevant existing evidence | [Buzzfy](https://buzzfy.co/) describes revising successful prior reels and using the creator's baseline | Revisit useful existing material and test meaningful variations. Do not inherit a daily volume quota or assume past reach guarantees future results. |
| Investigate what the audience needs, beyond competitor popularity | YouTube documents [audience viewing reports](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314416?hl=en) and [search/content-gap discovery](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11962757?hl=en) | Use available first-party signals to find questions, relevant formats, and underserved topics. Availability varies; unavailable account data stays unknown, and search interest is not purchase intent. |
| Define the outcome behind a success claim | [Runneth's methodology](https://runneth.com/thumbstop-pulse/cb2026-methodology-and-definitions) defines winners using realized spend | Check whether a source measures delivery, attention, engagement, attributable action, or business value before borrowing its benchmark. Keep paid and organic evidence distinct. |

These are candidate practices, not universal growth promises. Stronger reach can accompany weak fit, and useful community or educational work may have a different goal from sales.

## Integration: social-marketer

Version 1.3.0 implements project-specific social marketing research as a distinct role. Social-content now focuses on post copy, scripts, repurposing, calendars, and focused variants; project research and strategic decisions belong to social-marketer. Both remain usable independently, without requiring a research audit for every post.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| social-marketer | Audience and market research, channel rationale, positioning for social, campaign objectives, strategic experiments, and outcome review |
| social-content | Turn the direction into topics, hooks, captions, scripts, repurposing, and an editorial calendar |
| social-designer | Produce and inspect static quote posts, carousels, covers, and related visuals |
| video-editor | Produce and inspect reels, explainers, animated quotes, talking-head edits, and longer video |

The working method is maintained in social-marketer's `references/project-research.md`, accessible through the plugin README. It establishes the project foundation, refreshes evidence for meaningful decisions, separates facts/inferences/hypotheses, and retains a sourced strategy and usable production brief. Research and strategy documents belong with the active project; the shared brand brief remains authoritative for brand facts.

Broader positioning/PMM skills may contribute when available; they are optional rather than mandatory dependencies. Goals may include education, community, trust, or sales. Research and plans do not authorize publishing, account connections, messaging, or ad spend. Evidence of improved marketing performance remains pending actual use and audience results.
