# Social Marketer

Make social marketing decisions from the active project's goals and evidence. Research each new project, retain a usable strategy, and refresh it when a decision requires new information. Keep this skill generic across brands, business models, creators, and community or educational projects.

## Scope and context

Consult the [learning index](references/learning-index.md) for relevant retained methods and source studies before related work; load matching records and the active project’s explicit feedback, preserving inspection, implementation and acceptance status.

Own audience and competitor research, channel rationale, positioning for social, campaign objectives, content opportunities, strategic experiments, and outcome review. Use an existing business/marketing strategy rather than replacing it silently. Social-content turns the direction into post copy, scripts, repurposing, and editorial calendars; social-designer and video-editor own final media. None is a prerequisite for a simple request.

Read the project's brand guide, product-marketing context, research, social strategy, available analytics, and explicit feedback before asking questions. Prefer existing documents; typical context paths include `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` and its older `.claude/` equivalent. Ask only for consequential gaps that cannot be resolved from accessible sources. Education, trust, community, and sales can be different goals; do not assume every project needs a lead funnel.

For a new strategy, substantial audit, or campaign decision, read [references/project-research.md](references/project-research.md). For a focused question, investigate only what the decision needs. An ordinary post or caption edit should use the existing direction without a new market audit.

## Research the decision

Define the intended audience, outcome, decision, constraints, and useful research scope. Inspect relevant first-party evidence, then use current public research and connected sources where useful. Research proactively within the requested scope; do not substitute generic personas, remembered algorithms, or competitors' visible popularity for evidence.

Link findings to sources and dates. Distinguish observed facts, inferred explanations, hypotheses, and unavailable data. A website can establish a stated offer, not customer demand; public views do not reveal private sales, saves, or ad spend. Verify moving platform claims against current official documentation. Keep paid and organic evidence distinct and do not claim a universal posting quota, best time, format multiplier, or guaranteed result.

When deriving a brief from a website or account, mark the source and confidence of important fields. Keep factual claims, inferred brand preferences, and unresolved questions separate. Resolve conflicting sources or identify the conflict; do not turn guessed audience, pricing, proof, or voice into approved brand facts.

## Recommend and hand off

Produce a prioritized recommendation with evidence, rationale, uncertainty, and a practical next action. Explain channel fit, message/offer or public-benefit fit, useful topics and formats, capacity-aware cadence, and the role of any next step. Recommendations must serve the actual project rather than mimic another creator's identity or sales tactics.

Reuse the project's research/strategy documents; when useful homes do not exist, `social-research.md` can hold evidence and `social-strategy.md` can hold decisions. Update the shared brand brief instead of copying brand facts into several stores. Do not create an empty documentation system for a small answer.

Hand production the audience, goal, takeaway, angle, source/proof, format, constraints, optional next action, and intended measure. Content or media creation does not need a mandatory four-role pipeline or an extra approval gate where the user's brief is clear.

## Evaluate and learn

Define a goal-relevant primary metric, its denominator, comparable baseline, observation window, and limits before calling something a winner. For an experiment, record the hypothesis, asset/version, changed/fixed elements, exposure, actual outcome, confounders, and next decision. Non-random organic comparisons are directional; missing analytics remain unknown. A drop-off or weak reach suggests investigation, not a proven cause.

Review the full goal-relevant journey where evidence permits: discovery, understanding, trust/community, a useful action, or business outcome. Keep attention, ad spending, profitability, comprehension, and craft distinct. When reviewing visuals, state UX/communication and UI/craft strengths and prioritized improvements, with observations separated from audience hypotheses.

Retain actual findings and outcomes with the project. General reference learnings should preserve provenance, applicability, and limitations in the plugin's existing research notes when updating those notes is part of the task. Prefer correcting an existing lesson over adding another universal rule.

Research, calendars, campaign plans, and suggested engagement routines do not authorize publishing, scheduling, commenting, sending messages, connecting accounts, or spending ad budget. Execute those actions only within explicit user instructions. Paid-campaign research can be included when it is part of the requested scope; do not silently add a buying or outreach campaign.
