# Social marketing learning index

Start with the project's current goal, research, strategy and actual audience evidence. Retrieve [project research](project-research.md) for the maintained decision workflow: source/date, audience signals, channel rationale, positioning, meaningful refresh triggers and production handoff.

Useful retrieval terms: audience questions, community, content gaps, competitors, paid versus organic, baseline, denominator, discovery-to-action journey, experiment, capacity and campaign outcome.

The broader source history (Buzzfy, One Peak, Ootto, Runneth and Joseph) is retained by social-content in its creative and social-marketing research notebooks when that companion/source checkout is available. Those studies contain vendor descriptions, documented mechanisms and candidate tactics, not guaranteed growth results. This standalone skill uses its own project-research guidance without requiring another skill package.

Keep actual strategy, private analytics and project outcomes in their existing project documents. Separate observed evidence, inferred explanations, proposed experiments and measured results. Reverify moving platform facts. Add a local pointer here when a new general marketing research record is retained; do not duplicate client facts or replace a project's current goal with an old campaign.
