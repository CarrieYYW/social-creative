## Synthesis for production

A useful starting library separates five decisions:

1. **Image role:** meaningful scene, botanical/detail, human gesture, object, abstract atmosphere or no image. Choose a text-bearing area and test the crop; darkening should preserve the image rather than hide an unsuitable source.
2. **Type role:** editorial serif, quiet sans, handwritten-style note, monospaced graphic statement. Specify weight, real italic availability, line breaks, width, leading, tracking, alignment and attribution hierarchy. Font identity was not verified from the accounts; do not label substitutes as exact matches.
3. **Material:** clean matte, fine photographic-looking grain, paper fibers or sparse dust. A blur, duotone, shadow or photographed material is not interchangeable with texture. Apply adjustable material to the background so live text stays crisp. Avoid a universal texture requirement.
4. **Composition:** centered quote, left-aligned block within a larger reading area, or image-led small text. Text size follows copy length and role. Use the same copy across first comparisons, then test alternate content.
5. **Time, for reels:** static full-thought overlay on moving scene, phrase sequence, interview captions or a designed animation. These require different timing and production routes. A still preview does not validate the motion. Freeze material randomness where a static material is intended; intentional moving grain needs its own playback check.

## Implemented experiment

The [quote studio](../assets/quote-studio/README.md) supplies five experimental presets, independent typography/background/material controls, live text, undo/redo, project save/restore, image retention/crop/softening and PNG/SVG exports. Atmosphere, Clean type, Handwritten note and Graphic poster were rendered with the same original short draft. Image & serif was technically exercised with a synthetic image; user-supplied creative imagery is still needed for that comparison.

Openly licensed Cormorant Garamond (including designed italics), Inter, Caveat and IBM Plex Mono supply substitute roles. The procedural material and abstract artwork are original implementations. No account's artwork, logo, quotation, identity or paid preset was copied into these generic defaults. Technical verification is recorded with the template; aesthetic approval remains open.

**UX:** presets offer a quick comparison while granular controls retain recovery/save/export. Irrelevant color/image controls are hidden or disabled. **UI:** the tested examples preserve separate type roles, intentional margins and crisp text over material. Image suitability, very small type, handwritten clarity and contrast after arbitrary edits require visual review; an export pass is not a universal contrast certification.
