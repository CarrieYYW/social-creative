# Static References and Reusable Formats

Use when the user supplies inspiration, asks what can be learned from a design, or wants a repeatable post/carousel format. An approved small edit can proceed without a separate reference analysis.

## Inspect and explain

Record the source, inspection date, actual coverage, format, intended audience, and message. Inspect the complete carousel when available. A cover alone does not establish the reading arc; public engagement counts do not establish comprehension, saves, or conversion.

Use slide/frame identifiers to connect findings to evidence. Examine both perspectives:

- **UX and communication:** subject clarity, reading order, context, progression, promised payoff, action clarity, and accessibility in use. State strengths and friction; distinguish observed problems from audience hypotheses.
- **UI and craft:** hierarchy, type scale and relationships, line breaks, spacing, alignment, contrast, imagery, attribution, and consistency at phone size. Explain which visual choices serve the message.

Extract a transferable relationship, such as a large statement followed by a quieter explanation or a recurring visual that develops across slides. Specify what to borrow, why it may fit, what to adapt, and what to leave behind. Avoid copying distinctive wording, logos, or a complete identity. Record unavailable slides and unverified performance claims explicitly.

## Turn a pattern into a format card

Keep reusable cards in the active project's existing reference library. A useful card names its purpose, audience, opening promise, progression, resolution, visual treatment, production needs, sources, and unsuitable contexts. Record actual outcomes separately from preferences and hypotheses.

Choose a structure for the message: a standalone quote, quote plus clearly separated commentary, comparison, sequence, or explanation. Count slides from the content. A static post does not need to become a carousel; a carousel does not need an arbitrary slide count or obligatory CTA.

Validate the card with real copy and a representative post or cover/content pair before extending the set. Preserve quote wording and attribution; distinguish sourced material from interpretation. Make imagery, typography, and space carry meaning rather than adding decoration to resemble a reference.

## Compare variants deliberately

For visual exploration, hold copy constant when comparing composition or typography. For a performance test, state the hypothesis, changed element, fixed elements, intended metric, and measurement limitations in the handoff. A cover-only test keeps the inner carousel stable; changing the entire treatment tests the package, not one visual property.

Inspect every actual variant for readability, consistency, and correct exports using [Static Social Design](static-design.md). Do not declare an engagement winner from aesthetic preference. When results arrive, retain the artifact, outcome, context, and next decision with the project. Detailed analytics can use social-content when available; this skill remains able to produce and review static assets independently.
