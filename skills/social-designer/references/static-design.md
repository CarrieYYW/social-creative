# Static Social Design

## Quote posts

Treat the quote as a composition, not body text pasted onto a background. Identify its central idea and natural phrasing; use line breaks, weight, scale, and space to establish an intentional reading path. Keep attribution legible and subordinate to the quote without making it disappear.

Preserve exact wording when presented as a direct quotation. Do not silently shorten, combine, or rewrite a quote for fit. If the passage is too long, use a different composition, a carousel, or an explicitly labeled excerpt agreed within the task. For paraphrases or original brand copy, label their status accurately and avoid false attribution.

Choose the visual treatment from meaning and brand context. Photography can establish a concrete situation; illustration can explain a relationship; texture can create atmosphere; a type-led design can give a strong sentence room. Avoid ornamental imagery that competes with the message. Check text against the actual background, including its busiest areas.

Deliver quote mockups with editable text, attribution, typography, spacing, colors, and relevant imagery controls, plus a matching rendered preview. Follow [editable mockups](editable-mockups.md) for format selection, user controls, save/reopen verification, and export fidelity.

## Carousels

Define a complete reading arc: an opening that makes the topic clear, development that advances the idea, and an ending that resolves it. Use an invitation or CTA when it serves the brief. Choose the slide count from the amount and complexity of content; there is no universal best count.

Give each slide a clear role and a manageable amount of information. A quote carousel might present a full quote, an interpretation, and an application; an explainer might use a sequence, a comparison, or an example. Keep quotations distinct from commentary. Do not split a sentence across slides merely to manufacture a reveal.

Use a repeatable grid, consistent text roles, attribution treatment, and a restrained set of visual motifs. Change scale or composition when it helps a new beat. Repeating the same text block on every slide creates monotony; unrelated layouts undermine coherence.

Inspect a contact sheet for overall consistency and the slides in order for comprehension and rhythm. Then inspect each export at phone viewing size. Check that later slides remain understandable when seen individually, without bloating them with repeated context.

## Covers and thumbnails

Make the topic or promise clear at preview size. Select a meaningful subject or frame and a crop that remains recognizable. Short display text should complement the content rather than obscure it. Check feed/profile previews and full-size presentation separately when those surfaces are part of the destination.

## Adaptation and handoff

Treat an approved master as a set of relationships, not fixed coordinates. Recompose around the destination ratio, image focal point, text length, and preview crop. Recheck attribution and any action text after adaptation.

Use actual editor layers, a design file, or reproducible source according to available tooling. Preserve source copy, font choices, assets, and export settings with the project. Deliver numbered files for carousels and a clear distinction between review previews and final exports.

For a matching video, share reference frames and the message hierarchy. Animation timing and sound need a separate editorial treatment; a static slide sequence does not automatically become an effective reel.
