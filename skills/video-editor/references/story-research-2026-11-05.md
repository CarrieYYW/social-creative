# Story, hooks and sharing: research record, 2026-11-05

Status: desk research, requested by the user. Abstracts, publisher pages, first-party pages and secondary summaries were read as marked. No full-paper review of every source, no video playback study and no test on the user's accounts. Working method: [story and hooks](story-and-hooks.md).

## Evidence

## Conflict resolved

Google says introduce the brand early. Tellis et al. report that early brand prominence reduces sharing. They measure different goals: recall and sales versus sharing. The method makes this an explicit choice per video.

## Claims checked and not adopted

| Claim | Why not |
|---|---|
| "You have 3 seconds" as a hard rule | Found only in vendor and marketing blogs. Early seconds matter, but no primary source supports a fixed cutoff. Thresholds in Studio are labelled heuristics |
| "65% of people who watch 3 seconds watch 10" and "45% watch 30" | Attributed to Meta on blogs. No primary source found |
| "90% of ad recall is in the first six seconds" | Attributed to TikTok on blogs. Not confirmed |
| Sends weighted 3–5× likes; "3% sends is viral" | Traced to third parties. Reported wording is only "slightly more important" |
| Benchmarks such as "75–80% viewed" on Shorts | Sources conflict; no official threshold found |
| Fixed re-hooks or cuts every few seconds | Already rejected in this plugin; the Reels study points to moderate pace |
| Manufacturing anger or fear because it spreads | It may spread, but it is not an accepted method here |

Sources not retrieved first-hand: TikTok Creative Codes, MrBeast's production notes, Paddy Galloway's Shorts study, YouTube Help on viewed versus swiped away, and Meta's creative guidance. Jonah Berger's STEPPS framework informed the send table from background knowledge, not from a source read in this batch.

## What would strengthen this

Run the method on real projects and retain outcomes: opening variants with a fixed body, measured against a comparable baseline. Study reference videos with full playback rather than captions and sampled frames.
